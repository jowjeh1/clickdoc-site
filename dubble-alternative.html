Dubble publishes the largest free tier here, and this page is not going to pretend otherwise

Of the products on this site's comparison pages, Dubble publishes the largest free offer. Its pricing page states unlimited guides, unlimited sharing and exports, and redaction included at no cost, with Pro at $18 per user, per month. The other published free tiers are numbers you can hit: Clickdoc's 10 guides, Folge's 5 local guides, Tango's 5 shared workflows, Guidde's 25 videos. Scribe publishes no cap at all, so there is no number to compare it on. Dubble's is unlimited by its own words.

So if the question is "which one gives me the most for nothing", the answer on the published pages is Dubble, and no amount of framing changes that. Clickdoc is not the cheaper starting point and this page does not claim it is.

The question Clickdoc answers is a different one: where do the screenshots end up, and how many times do you pay? A hosted product has to hold your images to serve them back as a link, which is exactly what makes unlimited free sharing possible and exactly what some people cannot allow. Clickdoc keeps the pictures in browser storage on your own machine, exports a file you hand over yourself, and costs $49 once for one person or $149 once for five seats. Those two facts are the entire pitch.

Stay with Dubble if

  • You want unlimited free guides. Clickdoc's free tier stops at 10 and then asks for $49. Dubble's published free tier has no such number, so if the budget is zero and the guide count is high, Dubble is the better answer and this page will not argue with it.
  • You want hosted sharing. One link, kept current in one place, that anyone can open — including people outside your company, and without them installing anything. Clickdoc has no server, so there is no URL to send.

Both of those need somebody's storage and bandwidth, which is what the $18 per user, per month is paying for on the plans above the free tier. A tool sold for a single payment cannot fund a service that runs forever.

What Clickdoc does not do

  • No desktop-app capture. Chrome tabs only, on any site.
  • No AI-written step text. Steps come from the clicks and the typed input, and you edit the wording.
  • No cloud sharing and no hosted links. There is no server, so there is no URL to send — you hand over a file.
  • No team analytics. Nothing reports who opened a guide.
  • No video. Screenshots and text only.

Pick Clickdoc if

The screenshots are the problem. If what you are documenting is an internal admin console, a customer record, a finance system or anything else where a picture of the screen must not be uploaded to a company you do not have a contract with, an unlimited free hosted tier is not an offer you can accept. Clickdoc records in Chrome, keeps every image in browser storage on that machine, and exports one HTML file with the pictures inside it, Markdown, a rich copy for pasting, or a PDF.

The price works the same way. It is $49 once for one person or $149 once for five seats, with no renewal — the licence is checked once and then works offline. Over a year, one Dubble Pro seat at $18 per user, per month costs more than a Clickdoc licence you never pay for again; the trade you are making for that is the hosted link and everything built on it.
